The challenge

Hold times decide how travellers feel about an airline, yet nobody measures them properly.

Teams that depend on airline phone lines are flying blind. They learn about a bad day from complaints, not data.

Waits are invisible

Hold times swing from minutes to an hour across airlines, lines and times of day. Without continuous measurement there is no baseline and no early warning.

Manual test calls don’t scale

Someone dialing and waiting by hand gets one data point per hour at best, and their time is spent listening to hold music instead of doing their job.

No evidence to act on

When a service level slips, there are no recordings, transcripts or timestamps to show what happened, so conversations with partners stay anecdotal.

FAQ

Questions we usually get

Is the demo connected to real airline calls?

No. The live demo runs on sample data and a call simulation so you can try every screen. Real calling starts in the pilot, on hotlines we agree together.

How accurate is human detection?

Accuracy depends on each airline’s hold audio. We measure it during the pilot against manually reviewed calls and tune the threshold per hotline. The target is 95% or better.

Does it tie up airline agents?

Only for a few seconds. Once a person answers, Call Flow thanks them and ends the call, and volume limits per hotline keep the load minimal.

Can we use our own telephony provider?

Yes. Twilio and Telnyx are supported out of the box, and other providers can be added during production setup.

Who owns the data?

You do. Call records, recordings and analytics stay in your workspace, can be exported at any time, and are deleted on request.

Can it be branded or extended for our team?

Yes. Cplusoft designs and builds the product, so custom dashboards, integrations and branding can be scoped into the production phase.
